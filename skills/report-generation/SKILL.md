# Report Generation

Build a report that lets its audience act confidently and verify material claims.

## Workflow

1. Establish audience, decision to support, format, length, deadline, and source-of-truth materials.
2. Draft around conclusion, evidence, implications, recommendation, and next action; do not let source order dictate the report.
3. Attribute material facts, calculations, quotations, and recommendations. Label inference and uncertainty clearly.
4. Store drafts and intermediate tables in `workspace/`; write final deliverables to `output/`.

## Quality gate

Verify factual consistency, arithmetic, citations/links, dates, terminology, requested format, and that the executive summary matches the body. Deliver the final path plus assumptions and unresolved risks.
