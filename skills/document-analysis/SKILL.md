# Document Analysis

Extract operational meaning and make every important conclusion traceable.

## Workflow

1. Confirm document type, scope, version/date, and whether referenced appendices are present.
2. Map purpose, actors, definitions, requirements, decisions, deadlines, dependencies, exceptions, and risks.
3. Quote or cite the smallest supporting section for material claims. Keep facts distinct from interpretation.
4. Compare documents only against requested dimensions; identify contradictions, missing controls, and ambiguous language.

## Output

Provide an executive answer first, then an evidence table where multiple claims need comparison. Include source locations and questions that cannot be resolved from the supplied material.
