# Research

Find the strongest available evidence for a bounded question; do not turn browsing into an unstructured link dump.

## Workflow

1. State the question, decision context, jurisdiction/timeframe, and what would count as sufficient evidence.
2. Prefer primary and authoritative sources. For volatile facts, verify recency and publication date; use independent sources for contested claims.
3. Capture claim, source, date, scope, and limitations. Distinguish source statements from inference.
4. Stop when the question is adequately answered or remaining uncertainty needs user direction; do not browse indefinitely.

## Output

Lead with the answer and confidence level. Summarize evidence, material caveats, and unresolved questions. Link each consequential claim to its supporting source and avoid unsupported generalizations.
