# Korean Prose Editing

Improve readability and natural Korean expression without changing what the author means or representing AI-generated work as human-authored.

## Edit with fidelity

1. Identify the audience, genre, desired formality, source-of-truth text, and whether the request is a draft, a light edit, or a substantial rewrite.
2. Preserve names, figures, dates, quotations, citations, claims, uncertainty, and the author's stated position. Flag an ambiguous or unsupported claim instead of silently replacing it.
3. Correct translationese, redundant modifiers, mechanical parallel lists, repeated sentence endings, unnecessary English glosses, and excessive headings or emphasis only when doing so improves the requested voice.

## Quality check

1. Read the revision as a whole for natural rhythm, terminology consistency, honorific/register consistency, and logical connective flow.
2. Compare material passages to the source. Report substantive wording changes, unresolved ambiguity, and any fact that needs verification.
3. Do not optimize for bypassing AI detectors, authorship concealment, plagiarism, or academic/workplace misrepresentation. Offer transparent editing assistance instead.
